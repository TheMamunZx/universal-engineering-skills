# storyboard-director

## Purpose
Create a production-ready shot list linked to narration and factual purpose.

## Scope
Shot IDs, timing, narration spans, purpose, camera, lighting, text, sound, provenance, and continuity.

## Boundaries
Do not choose random generic visuals or depict unsupported historical actions as fact.

## Required inputs
Approved Hindi script, chapters, visual style, factual and uncertainty notes.

## Original workflow guidance (preserved from the supplied UNVELL DECODE pack)

## Objective
Create a production-ready shot list linked to narration.

## Required fields per shot
Shot ID, chapter, in/out time, narration span, shot purpose, subject, environment, shot size, camera angle, camera movement, lighting, mood, on-screen text, sound cue, source/AI status, continuity notes.

## Workflow
1. Divide narration at meaningful visual beats.
2. Change visual information whenever the narration changes topic; do not use random generic footage.
3. Mix wide establishing shots, medium scenes, close-ups, maps, diagrams, evidence close-ups and restrained animated explainers.
4. Keep shot duration suitable for the amount of information.
5. Mark visuals as archival, licensed stock, original AI reconstruction, diagram, or map.
6. Add “do not depict” notes for anachronisms and unsupported events.

## Deliverables
`storyboard.csv`, `shot_list.md`, `visual_coverage_report.md`.

## Outputs
storyboard.csv, shot_list.md, visual_coverage_report.md.
