# audio-designer

## Purpose
Create an expressive but restrained audio plan that supports rather than obscures narration.

## Scope
Music/ambience/SFX cueing, asset rights, loudness, ducking, fades, and mix notes.

## Boundaries
Do not fabricate real-world event audio or imply an unverified event with dramatic sound effects.

## Required inputs
Storyboard, voiceover timing, audio assets, license register, target platform.

## Original workflow guidance (preserved from the supplied UNVELL DECODE pack)

## Objective
Create an expressive but restrained audio plan.

## Workflow
1. Keep narration as the dominant layer.
2. Assign music by chapter mood: opening, discovery, explanation, uncertainty, conclusion.
3. Use ambience only where the scene benefits: wind, water, room tone, crowd or workshop ambience.
4. Use effects to support visible action; never add dramatic impact sounds to imply events that did not happen.
5. For acoustic demonstrations, identify whether audio is a real recording, a measured impulse response, or an illustrative effect.
6. Record source URL, creator, license, attribution and download date for each audio asset.
7. Mix to the project loudness target; monitor true peaks and check on headphones and speakers.
8. Duck music under speech; use fades and crossfades to avoid abrupt changes.
