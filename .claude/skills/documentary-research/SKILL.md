# documentary-research

## Purpose
Build a reliable research dossier for a documentary topic.

## Scope
Source discovery, primary-source preference, atomic claim extraction, source limitations, chronology, and uncertainty tracking.

## Boundaries
Do not write a persuasive script, present hypotheses as verified facts, or invent citations.

## Required inputs
Topic, geography/time period, audience, duration, documentary language, supplied claims.

## Original workflow guidance (preserved from the supplied UNVELL DECODE pack)

## Objective
Build a reliable research dossier for a documentary topic.

## Required inputs
Topic, geography/time period, documentary language, intended duration, and any user-supplied claims.

## Workflow
1. Search official/primary sources first: archaeology departments, heritage authorities, museum catalogues, inscriptions/translations, excavation reports, peer-reviewed papers.
2. Add reputable secondary sources only to explain context.
3. Record each source's title, author/institution, date, URL, source type, relevant passage/page, access date and limitations.
4. Extract atomic claims. Do not combine several claims into one vague sentence.
5. Label each claim Verified, Supported but debated, Hypothesis, Unknown, or False/Misleading.
6. Record conflicting evidence and identify what cannot be established.
7. Create a concise timeline, key people/places, technical terms and visual opportunities.

## Deliverables
`research_notes.md`, `sources.csv`, `claim_register.csv`, `open_questions.md`.

## Quality gate
No scriptwriting handoff until every high-impact claim has a source or is explicitly marked as uncertainty.

## Outputs
research_notes.md, sources.csv, claim_register.csv, open_questions.md.

## Handoff
Coordinate with: fact-checker, engineering-researcher, hindi-scriptwriter. Do not duplicate their responsibilities; provide the named artifacts and a concise handoff note.

## Completion checks
- Deliverables exist at the stated paths; do not claim a file was created unless it is present.
- Separate verified facts, supported interpretations, hypotheses, and unknowns.
- Record blockers and missing inputs explicitly.
- Never expose credentials or start paid/bulk actions without explicit approval.
