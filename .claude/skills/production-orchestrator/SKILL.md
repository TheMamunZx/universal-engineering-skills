# production-orchestrator

## Purpose
Coordinate specialist skills and enforce research, approval, cost, rights, and delivery gates.

## Scope
Brief intake, stage sequencing, artifact tracking, approval gates, blockers, and final manifest.

## Boundaries
Do not bypass human approval for paid generation, publishing, uploading, or irreversible changes.

## Required inputs
Topic, duration, target audience, language, style, budget, tools/providers, output specs.

## Original workflow guidance (preserved from the supplied UNVELL DECODE pack)

## Objective
Coordinate specialist skills and enforce stage gates.

## Workflow
1. Read the project brief and studio config.
2. Ensure Research and Fact Check are complete before Script approval.
3. Ensure Script is approved before Storyboard.
4. Ensure Storyboard is approved before asset generation.
5. Ensure asset licenses and narration rights are recorded before editing.
6. Keep a progress file with owner, status, blocker and output path for each stage.
7. Do not trigger paid generation or large batch jobs without explicit approval.
8. Do not upload or publish; prepare a review package only.

## Deliverables
`production_status.md`, stage handoff notes and final delivery manifest.

## Outputs
production_status.md, stage handoff notes, final delivery manifest.

## Handoff
Coordinate with: all documentary skills as needed; use skill-orchestrator only for cross-domain selection. Do not duplicate their responsibilities; provide the named artifacts and a concise handoff note.

## Completion checks
- Deliverables exist at the stated paths; do not claim a file was created unless it is present.
- Separate verified facts, supported interpretations, hypotheses, and unknowns.
- Record blockers and missing inputs explicitly.
- Never expose credentials or start paid/bulk actions without explicit approval.
